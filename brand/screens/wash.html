Rendered from the tree the Wash component produced under the test runner, at 390 by 844 points, and not captured from a phone. Every colour is read from @emi/tokens, which is held to the wash block of docs/design/prototype-design-system.md. Each panel is one wash over her own ground, so the band where the wash reaches the ground is the join and not an edge in the wash. A browser draws a circle where react-native-svg draws an ellipse, so each tint is written as the unit circle under a transform carrying its two radii: the shape is the one the component asked for, in another coordinate system. No screen of the application draws the wash yet. Reproduce with: npm run generate:wash-picture.
