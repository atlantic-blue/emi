Common questions.

I lost my phone. Can you restore my records?

Only with your recovery code. We do not hold a key, so we cannot restore anything without it.

Why does Emi say it is still learning?

Emi needs 2 complete cycles before it forecasts. Until then it counts by the cycle length you give it.

How do I delete everything?

Open Privacy in the app and choose Delete data. Or delete the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

If you paid in the App Store, cancel in your Apple account settings. If you paid on this site, cancel on your profile.
