Who holds the keys. Not Emi.

Your phone

The vault key encrypts every day. It never leaves the phone.

You

A recovery code of 26 characters, shown once. Keep it away from the phone.

Emi

No key. So no request to Emi, or to Amazon Web Services, can return one of your days.

What Emi does not defend against.

Four limits, stated here and not hidden. Each one says what you can do.

A lost phone and a lost code

The days are gone. An Emi that could recover them could read them.

A phone somebody can unlock

They can read the screen. Keep the app lock on. It is on by default.

A phone running something hostile

No app can defend itself from under the system. Keep iOS current.

What the outside learns

With sync, the server learns how many records you keep, and when each one was written.
