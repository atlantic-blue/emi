Four rows: her answers, the lock, the export and the way out. Her answers is faded because nothing opens behind it yet, and it carries no line promising a screen that is absent.

Rendered from the tree the screen produced under the test runner, at 390 by 844 points, and not captured from a phone. The page loads the same font files the application loads, so the words are drawn in Plus Jakarta Sans. The room kept at the top and the bottom is the room an iPhone with a dynamic island keeps for itself, which is 59 points and 34 points. The dock is not drawn, because the screen is rendered on its own rather than inside the navigator. Reproduce with: npm run generate:privacy-picture.
