The promise

Three lines, each one a thing the product does today. Nothing about the hardware the key sits in, and no standard anybody has checked Emi against.

Only you can read your days.
Encrypted on this phone, with a key only you hold
Emi encrypts each day on the phone, with a key that stays on the phone, and sends only the result.
No password, no tracking tools
Emi asks for no password and carries no tracking tools.
Delete everything, in one press
Delete everything removes every day from this phone.
