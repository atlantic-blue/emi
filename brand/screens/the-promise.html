The promise

Three lines, each one a thing the product does today. Nothing about the hardware the key sits in, and no standard anybody has checked Emi against.

Only you can read your days.
Encrypted with a key only you hold
Every day you log is locked on this phone, and only the locked copy is sent.
No password, no trackers
We don't ask for a password, and Emi carries no tracking tools.
Delete it all, any time
One press removes every day from this phone.
