Rendered from the trees the screens produced under the test runner, at 390 by 844 points, and not captured from a phone. The page loads the same font files the application loads, so the words are drawn in Plus Jakarta Sans. The room kept at the top and the bottom is the room an iPhone with a dynamic island keeps for itself, which is 59 points and 34 points. The answers are the ones a fixture carries, and not a profile read off a device. A wheel is drawn at its top, because the markup carries no scroll position, and a screen taller than the glass draws past the frame for the same reason: on a phone the middle scrolls and Save stays on the bottom edge.
