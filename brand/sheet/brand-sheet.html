The ring, in three states
A gap of ground and a written name carry the boundary. Colour is the third cue.
3
Period
A short cycle, 21 days
Day 3, still bleeding. Four bleeding days, and no room for a follicular phase.
14
Ovulation
A usual cycle, 28 days
Day 14. Five bleeding days, then seven, seven and nine.
40
Luteal
A long cycle, 45 days
Day 40. Twenty follicular days, which is where the extra length goes.

The illustrations
Two or three soft shapes in the phase colours. No body, no face, no flower, no droplet, no blood.
welcome
three colours crossing, so the whole cycle is one movement rather than four states
last-period
one shape settling into a wider one, so a start reads as a moment inside something longer
cycle-length
three shapes of one size at one spacing, so a length reads as a rhythm she can count
