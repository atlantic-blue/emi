Privacy policy

We cannot read your records. This page says why.

Who we are

Emi is made by Atlantic Blue Solutions Limited.

What Emi stores

Emi stores the dates, symptoms, moods and sex that you log. They stay on your phone. Emi encrypts each day before it writes it, with a key kept in your iPhone keychain. How the encryption works ›

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We cannot read your records.

Sharing

Your records leave your phone only when you export a file. You choose where the file goes.

Reminders and Face ID

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them. iOS handles Face ID, so Emi never sees your face data.

Deleting

Delete Emi, or use Delete my data in the app. Either one removes everything on the phone.

Children

Emi is not made for children under 13.

Contact and changes

Send privacy questions to privacy@myemi.app. When this policy changes, the date at the top changes.
